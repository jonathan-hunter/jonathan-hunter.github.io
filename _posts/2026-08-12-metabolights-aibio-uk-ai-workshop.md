Come visit EBI and join the discussion on the use of AI to further the metabolomics field.

Personally, I'll be looking to learn and contribute in some current areas of interest, including:

- Automated dataset metadata annotation, either at submission or during retrospective curation.
- The role of semantics, ontologies/controlled vocabularies, and knowledge graphs in enabling accurate AI accelerated metabolomics.
- The intersection between AI and chemistry, specifically effective chemical structure and name handling.
- Current methods in molecular/spectral networking, parameterless peak-picking, and in silico spectral prediction.

This is a timely and well-targeted workshop, co-located with the ELIXIR metabolomics community meeting. I anticipate a highly productive meeting driven by a critical mass of scientists, developers, informaticians and more, bringing experiences from a cross-section of scientific domains. Registration is open (link below) - I hope to see you there!

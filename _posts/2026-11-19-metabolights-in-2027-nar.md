Our paper describing the last three years of development in MetaboLights is now out in _Nucleic Acids Research_.

It covers usage statistics and recent updates including the new submission workflow, Globus transfer for very large datasets, the automated study validation framework and current trends in data reuse, alongside our plans for metadata capture, coordination across resources through the MetabolomicsHub, and AI.
